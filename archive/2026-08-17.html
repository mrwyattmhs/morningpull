GM & Cadillac performance

Cadillac V-Series & GM Performance

Cadillac revealed the V-ONE — a concept study combining the brand's race-winning authenticity and bespoke Cadillac refinement, unveiled at The Quail in Monterey on August 14th. The V-One concept shares its foundation with the V-Series.R race car and has an 830-horsepower hybrid V8 engine. Unlike the vinyl-wrapped V-Series.R on which it's based, the Cadillac V-ONE Concept gets a paint job hand-painted in silver at the front, fading to Caspia dark blue at the rear.

Cadillac Racing has achieved 10 wins, 16 pole positions and 32 podiums since the inception of the V-Series.R in 2023, with results including the 2023 IMSA WeatherTech Championship titles and Cadillac's first 1–2 finish in the FIA World Endurance Championship at São Paulo.

No major pricing, recalls, or production announcements for V-Series road cars emerged this week.
