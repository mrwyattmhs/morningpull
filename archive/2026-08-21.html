Cadillac V-Series.R Factory Motorsport

Current Season Status The updated V-Series.R turned its wheels for the first time at the IMSA season-opening Rolex 24 at Daytona, with entries from Action Express and Wayne Taylor Racing. The 2026 season is active in both IMSA GTP and WEC Hypercar classes.

Technical Updates for 2026 The V-Series.R will compete in the FIA World Endurance Championship and IMSA WeatherTech SportsCar Championship with a new aerodynamic setup, including removal of the front dive planes and winglets and a reshaped rear wing profile. I'll search for recent science news with a focus on space, physics, and engineering.## Top Science News: Space, Physics & Engineering

SpaceX Breaks Launch Record SpaceX launched back-to-back Falcon 9 rockets on Aug. 15 from opposite coasts, with the USSF-366 booster returning to Earth about 8.5 minutes after launch to land on a droneship, now with 18 missions under its belt. The doubleheader brought the tally of Falcon 9 launches in 2026 to 96. Why it matters: The 38-minute gap between launches beat the previous record by 27 minutes, demonstrating SpaceX's operational cadence at unprecedented scale for commercial spaceflight.

China's First Stainless-Steel Booster Recovery This marks the world's first stainless-steel booster recovery following an orbital launch, beating SpaceX's Super Heavy to this milestone. Why it matters: Stainless steel is a relatively new approach in reusable rocket design; this achievement shows China's LandSpace advancing recovery technology independent of established players.

NASA's GPS-Free Navigation Test NASA's Starling mission tests FALCON, a system that lets spacecraft navigate without GPS. Why it matters: Autonomous navigation without ground-based GPS would be critical for deep-space missions and operations in GPS-denied environments.

Firefly and Zeno Nuclear Lunar Technology Firefly and Zeno will test nuclear technology designed to protect spacecraft during prolonged lunar darkness. Why it matters: Surviving the harsh lunar night (14+ days) with power and thermal protection is essential for sustained human presence on the Moon.

Novel Spacecraft Attitude Control The Supertorquer is a world-first system that uses superconducting electromagnetics to control a spacecraft's attitude. Why it matters: Superconducting attitude control could offer more precise, power-efficient spacecraft maneuvering than traditional systems.

NASA's Swift Observatory Rescue Mission In mid-2026, NASA's Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory is planned to be boosted to a higher orbit by a private Arizona-based firm, Katalyst Space Technologies, using the LINK spacecraft launched on a Northrop Grumman Pegasus XL aircraft-borne rocket. Why it matters: A first-of-its-kind commercial rescue of an aging but still-productive space telescope—demonstrating a new business model for extending observatory lifespans.

Quantum Gravitational Wave Detection A new NASA Innovative Advanced Concepts (NIAC) grant is funding a concept from a team led by Paul Stankus at Brookhaven National Laboratory that could potentially solve engineering problems in gravitational wave detection by using quantum mechanics. Why it matters: Quantum approaches could dramatically improve sensitivity of gravitational wave detectors, opening new windows on cosmic events.

All-Optical Photonic Time Crystal Scientists have built the first all-optical photonic time crystal, allowing them to reshape the behavior of terahertz light at extraordinary speeds. The breakthrough could open the door to ultrafast computing, smarter communication systems, advanced... Why it matters: A photonic time crystal is an entirely new class of material with potential applications in next-generation computing and signal processing.
