Cadillac V-Series & GM Performance

The Cadillac V-One Concept packs 830 horsepower and V-Series.R technology into a customer track car. Cadillac currently has no plans to build it, but a serialized plaque on the door teases the company's intention to make more copies for sale.

The 2026 Lyriq-V begins production later this year as the first electric Cadillac to wear the V-Series badge. It produces 615 horsepower and 650 lb-ft of torque, hitting 0-60 mph in 3.3 seconds in Velocity Max mode.

The Cadillac V-Series.R race car is receiving aerodynamic upgrades for 2026 that will improve track grip and reduce drag for the IMSA and FIA World Endurance Championship circuits.
