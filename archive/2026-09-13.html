Track driving & car control

TECHNIQUE & DRIVER EDUCATION

HPDE sessions rotate between track driving and educational briefings covering braking technique, cornering, vehicle dynamics, and racing line fundamentals. Events organize drivers into skill groups with novice participants attending classroom sessions and receiving in-car instruction from experienced coaches. Track days teach track etiquette, safety, and basic performance driving techniques such as cornering, braking, and acceleration.

TIRES FOR TRACK USE

2026 Testing Results:

The Nankang CR-S showed extreme consistency and thermal stability, not quite matching the peak first-lap grip of competitors but staying in its performance window much longer and remaining easier to push.

In wet braking from 100 km/h, the Michelin Pilot Sport Cup 2 stopped shortest, with the Yokohama Advan A052 just 0.2 metres behind. The Yokohama Advan A052's soft compound paid off in wet conditions.

Top high-performance tires for 2026 include the Michelin Pilot Sport S 5, Continental ExtremeContact Sport 02, Bridgestone Potenza Sport, Michelin Pilot Sport 4 S, and Continental SportContact 7, offering exceptional wet and dry performance with precise handling.

BRAKES FOR TRACK

The FNSL6R caliper offers a proven design in club racing and track day applications with excellent heat management and consistent pedal feel through long sessions. Big brake kits are essential for HPDE, time attack, and lapping days where consistent braking is critical.

DATA & COACHING TOOLS

Real-Time Telemetry:

Real-time lap comparison shows where on track you're falling behind or gaining time, allowing race coaches to tune driver performance while on track without offline data downloads. RaceCapture systems provide 3-axis G-force plus Yaw/Pitch/Roll measurements for analyzing cornering forces and detecting oversteer/understeer.

Sim Racing Tools (applicable to real-world coaching):

Delta's AI coaching analyzes telemetry in real time, breaking down each corner into Braking, Entry, Apex, and Exit phases to identify exactly where drivers lose time. Track Titan's Coaching Flows feature guides drivers through their biggest mistakes with claimed average improvements of half a second after first session analysis.

Popular telemetry analysis tools include MoTeC, VRS, Atlas, and SimRacing Telemetry.

CAR PREPARATION FOR TRACK

You don't need a race car; all that's required is a street car meeting basic ASE safety requirements. Tech inspection ensures your car is safe for track use, and you must wear appropriate safety gear including a helmet.

Common setup approaches: stock wheels with street performance tires (PS4S), avoiding Cup2Rs that wear too quickly, with some drivers planning Vitour P-01R upgrades.
