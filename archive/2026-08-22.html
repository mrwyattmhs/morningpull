Track Driving & High-Performance Driver Education

TECHNIQUE & FUNDAMENTALS

HPDE events focus on learning, with drivers receiving instruction and participating in controlled environments designed to help them become faster, smoother, and safer behind the wheel. Drivers rotate between track sessions and educational briefings, learning concepts such as braking technique, cornering, vehicle dynamics, and racing line fundamentals.

TIRES FOR TRACK USE

Recent tire testing reveals several strong options for track days. Top high-performance tires for sports cars in 2026 include the Michelin Pilot Sport S 5, Continental ExtremeContact Sport 02, Bridgestone Potenza Sport, Michelin Pilot Sport 4 S, and Continental SportContact 7, offering exceptional wet and dry performance with precise handling and advanced technologies. Top track-ready tires that still feel good on the street include Continental ExtremeContact Sport 02, Michelin Pilot Sport Cup 2, and Goodyear Eagle F1 SuperCar 3.

Competition tires are built with motorsport-grade compounds, stronger sidewalls, and smarter tread patterns, giving consistent grip, sharper steering response, and control.

BRAKES FOR TRACK

The FNSL6R caliper is a proven design in club racing and track day applications, offering excellent heat management and consistent pedal feel through long sessions.

DATA & COACHING TOOLS

Garmin Catalyst 2, announced February 2026, is a compact motorsports device that helps high-performance drivers find faster times on the track with on-track coaching features, including True Optimal Lap which shows drivers their best achievable time with a composite video of lines they drove, real-time audio cues, and post-session comparison of performance data.

NOTABLE EVENTS

Hooked on Driving runs HPDE events at over 25+ track venues coast-to-coast including Watkins Glen, Laguna Seca, and Thunderhill. Pocono Raceway hosted two HPDE Track Days in 2026, with events on July 8th and September 9th.

CAR PREPARATION

HPDE requirements include NASA membership, any car in safe operating condition, mufflers, roll bars in convertibles, seat belts in good working order, state driver's license, long sleeve shirt and pants, and an approved helmet.
