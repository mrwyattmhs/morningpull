Enthusiast car market

ENTHUSIAST & COLLECTOR CAR MARKET

Auction Records & Recent Sales

Monterey Car Week 2026 shattered records with $755.6M in total sales, surpassing even the pandemic-fueled 2022 peak. Notable top results include:

• 1964 Shelby Cobra Daytona Coupe: $42.9M (record for an American car) • 1963 Chevrolet Corvette Grand Sport: $18.7M (record for a Corvette) • 1996 McLaren F1 GTR: $34.7M (record for a British car) • 1935 Duesenberg Model JN: $9.1M

Mecum's January Kissimmee sale reached $445M (doubling the prior year), while Arizona's January and March Amelia Island/Miami auctions combined for $255.9M, up from $195.1M the year before.

The Market's Tale of Two Tiers

The market is sharply diverging between cars collectors desperately want and everything else, with the clearest story of 2026 being the continued acceleration of this divergence. Modern low-production Ferraris—particularly those with halo status—commanded prices that would have seemed implausible a few years ago.

However, everyday classics are left trying to keep pace with inflation while supercars and blue-chip icons keep setting records.

Appreciation & Depreciation

• Modern classics (2000s+) were the only segment to see price rises, with examples like the 2000 Honda S2000 AP1 (+37.5%), 1999 Nissan Skyline R34 (+41%), and 2000 BMW Z8 (+25%). • Mass-market collectibles are generally falling in value; the Hagerty Hundred (weighted-average of 100 most insured vehicles) peaked at over $50K in May 2022 but has fallen to $43,408. • A Series I Jaguar E-Type flat-floor that previously traded around $300,000 sold for approximately $128,000.

Modern Performance Cars Trending Toward Collectibility

Collectors prioritize experience: cars delivering distinct driving experiences, mechanical engagement, and cultural identity perform far better than pure historical artifacts. The Porsche Carrera GT, Ferrari Enzo, Lamborghini Miura SV, and Diablo SE30 continue strong results—analog, expressive, and deeply authored vehicles.

Toyota Supras, Nissan Skylines, Mazda RX-7s, Acura Integras, Mitsubishi Evolutions, Subaru WRXs, and Honda performance models continue generating strong interest from younger collectors.

Hagerty's 2026 Bull Market List "shifts gears into the '90s and '00s," highlighting the growing appeal of modern speed, manual transmissions, and limited electronic interference.

I'll search for recent mathematics news across several areas.## Mathematics News & Highlights

Major Breakthroughs

AI Solves Long-Standing Problems

In 2026, a collaboration between the Institute for Advanced Study and DeepMind used the AI system LeanMind to prove the Sylvester-Gallai conjecture—the first major open problem solved by AI without substantial human guidance. The proof was formally verified in Coq and published in the Annals of Mathematics. Why it matters: This exposes the gap between classical mathematical practice—where proofs are often accepted on human intuition—and the new reality where machine-generated proofs demand formal verification to be trusted.

An AI-generated proof disproves the Erdős unit distance conjecture, demonstrating that for infinitely many values of n, the maximum number of unit-distance pairs among n points in the plane can exceed n1+δ for some δ > 0. Why it matters: For nearly 80 years, mathematicians have struggled to solve a classic geometry puzzle first posed by Paul Erdős in 1946.

Century-Old Hadamard Conjecture Resolved

A team from Oxford and MIT used a combination of combinatorial design theory and quantum annealing to construct explicit Hadamard matrices for all orders up to 1000, and then extended the method to prove the conjecture for all orders. Why it matters: The Hadamard conjecture—which posits that a Hadamard matrix exists for every order divisible by 4—remained open for over a century. Hadamard matrices are crucial for experimental design and error-correcting codes.

Major Prizes & Honors

2026 Fields Medals (awarded July 23, 2026)

The International Mathematical Union awarded four Fields Medals at the International Congress of Mathematicians in Philadelphia. They went to Hong Wang, Yu Deng, John Pardon and Jacob Tsimerman. Wang is the third woman to receive the medal since it was first awarded in 1936. Each closed a problem that had been open for decades, and in two cases for more than a century.

2026 Abel Prize

The Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters awarded the 2026 Abel Prize to Gerd Faltings, director emeritus at the Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in Bonn. This prestigious honor recognizes Faltings as the first German researcher to receive the award. Why it matters: His 1983 proof of the Mordell conjecture solved a 60-year-old puzzle and earned him the Fields Medal in 1986. His proof has had far-reaching implications for the eventual proof of Fermat's Last Theorem.

2025 Breakthrough Prize in Mathematics

Dennis Gaitsgory received the 2025 Breakthrough Prize in Mathematics for his central role in the proof of the geometric Langlands conjecture, described as the "grand unified theory of mathematics." This central component of a broader research program resulted in a proof totaling more than 800 pages and marked the culmination of 30 years of work.

Mathematics Education

National Focus on Math Reform

States are expanding opportunity for students by enacting policies that focus on improving math outcomes, which are tied to economic success. Only 39% of fourth graders and 27% of eighth graders meet the National Assessment of Educational Progress (NAEP) standards in math.

Alabama has demonstrated itself as a national leader in math policy with its Numeracy Act, which led the state to return to pre-pandemic proficiency levels. Alabama, Maryland and Indiana have already taken steps to reform math instruction.

Personalized Learning Models

The Collaboratory to Advance Mathematics Education and Learning (CAMEL) for K-12 initiative aims to advance mathematics learning through collaboration drawing on cognitive science, computer science, machine learning, and education research.
