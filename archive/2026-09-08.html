Enthusiast car market

Collector Car Market Update

Monterey Auction Results (August 2026)

A record $748 million was spent at Monterey Car Week 2026 auctions. Top results at Broad Arrow's The Quail sale included a 2003 Ferrari Enzo and a 2025 Bugatti Mistral Roadster. A 1964 Shelby Cobra Daytona Coupe set a record at Gooding Christie's.

Market Bifurcation: Strong Top, Weak Middle

Strength in high-end cars pushed the Hagerty Market Rating to a six-month high, despite softness in most of the classic car market. Average sale prices climbed 25% to $75,173 in the first half of 2026, while the median barely moved from $25,550 to $26,500—a widening gap showing concentration at the top.

For vehicles valued under $250,000, Hagerty member requests to increase insurance values rose only once in the past 15 months, while high-end vehicles saw increases eight times in the same timeframe.

Prestige Marques Dominate

Nine of the ten top auction sales in H1 2026 were Ferraris, accounting for 43% of every $1M+ sale industry-wide—114 seven-figure cars worth $543 million, more than triple the next-closest make (Porsche, at 13.6%).

1990s/2000s Gaining Ground

Modern enthusiast cars from the 1990s and 2000s are gaining popularity quickly, especially cars with analog appeal, manual transmissions, and limited electronic interference. Notable performers: 2000 Honda S2000 AP1 (+37.5%), 1999 Nissan Skyline R34 (+41%), 2000 BMW Z8 (+25%).

Hagerty's 2026 Bull Market List values the 1995–1998 Nissan Skyline GT-R at $82,350, 2006–2013 Corvette Z06 at $55,900, and 2004–2007 Porsche Carrera GT at $1.55 million. A Porsche Carrera GT sold at Amelia auction for around $6.7 million.

Depreciation Zone

British and Italian marques experienced declines: Jaguar (-22%), Aston Martin (-16%), Alfa Romeo (-22%), Maserati (-14%).

Why the Shift

High-performance supercars of the '90s and later are drawing more interest from younger buyers. The strongest performers are vehicles that represent the end of an era, a technical turning point or an experience modern cars can no longer replicate.
