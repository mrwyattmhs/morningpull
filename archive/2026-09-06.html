Cadillac racing

CADILLAC V-SERIES.R MOTORSPORT

2026 Season Overview

The updated V-Series.R competes in IMSA's GTP class with entries from Action Express Racing and Wayne Taylor Racing, while Hertz Team Jota fields a pair of revised prototypes in the WEC Hypercar category.

Le Mans

Three Cadillac V-Series.R race cars are entered in the 2026 24 Hours of Le Mans. Jack Aitken, Earl Bamber, Sébastien Bourdais, Alex Lynn, Norman Nato and Will Stevens will be the drivers. The No. 101 Cadillac Wayne Taylor Racing V-Series.R will compete at Le Mans for the second time. Ricky Taylor, Jordan Taylor, and Filipe Albuquerque will share driving duties in this race car.

WEC/IMSA Driver Lineups

WEC (Full Season): - Alex Lynn, Norman Nato, and Will Stevens will return to drive the No. 12 V-Series.R entry, having scored points in every race and placed fifth in the overall championship in 2025. - Jack Aitken joins the No. 38 Cadillac Racing team as an IMSA GTP endurance champion, supporting Earl Bamber and Sebastien Bourdais.

IMSA (Full Season): - Cadillac Wayne Taylor Racing No. 10: Ricky Taylor, Filipe Albuquerque (full season) with Will Stevens for Daytona, Sebring, Road Atlanta. No. 40: Jordan Taylor, Louis Deletraz (full season) with Colton Herta for Daytona, Sebring, Road Atlanta. - Jack Aitken, Earl Bamber and Frederick Vesti pilot the Action Express No. 31.

Technical Developments for 2026

For 2026, Cadillac updated its LMDh-spec prototype with revised aerodynamics focused on cutting drag and trimming away excess downforce, with the front end now devoid of diveplanes, but gaining a reprofiled splitter. A full hardware switch to Brembo brakes was also confirmed, after previously using only Brembo calipers for the past three seasons.

Recent Results

In the 2025 WEC Hypercar competition, Cadillac finished fourth in the manufacturers' championship and in July in Brazil the No. 12 Cadillac Hertz Team JOTA V-Series.R won the event, while the No. 38 car was second.

Guitar gear & players

GUITARS

Gibson, IK Multimedia, ESP and Ibanez headline recent releases. Epiphone launched its Inspired by Gibson lineup with all-solid, Fishman-loaded Hummingbirds, J-45s, and L-00s featuring mahogany and spruce tonewoods, bone nuts, and rosewood fingerboards. Epiphone revived the cult-classic Gibson RD guitar as part of its Futura series, with artist models in development for Lzzy Hale and Alex Lifeson. Taylor Next Generation introduced 12-fret models with more compact size and an unusual headstock design.

AMPS & MODELERS

IK Multimedia released a $999 modeler featuring AI Machine Modeling, AmpliTube and TONEX effects, ToneNET community integration, and a powerful customizable signal chain. Vox debuted new AC30 handwired head and cab options with Alnico Blue or Greenback speakers, alongside vintage-voiced fuzz and treble booster pedals. Orange rolled out the OR60—an all-valve guitar amp head described as the most versatile it has ever built.

PEDALS

Blackstar's digital amp gained Moises support for stem separation technology, available now on iOS with Android arriving September 30. Walrus Audio released the Canvas Expression pedal with two independent outputs configurable as expression, switch and CV outputs, fully customizable with MIDI controller capability. Jam Pedals released Stu Mackenzie's signature stompbox, the Tiny Amp Destroyer. EHX announced the Big Muff 2, based on a previously unreleased early dual op-amp '70s Big Muff circuit.

ARTIST NEWS

Alex Lifeson's "Fifty Something" tour rig has gone completely ampless, ditching traditional amps entirely and showing modern guitar tech evolution for touring professionals. Billy Gibbons remembered late ZZ Top drummer Frank Beard.

Blackwing & V-Series

CT4-V and CT5-V Blackwing Production

The 2026 Cadillac CT4 production will run through the end of June 2026, while the 2026 Cadillac CT5 will continue until the end of the 2026 calendar year. The 2026 model year is the last opportunity to acquire a new, current-generation CT5-V Blackwing from the factory. The last opportunity to place orders for the entire CT4 lineup is the week of April 20, 2026.

For its final model year, Cadillac is offering a "Curated by Cadillac" program allowing a limited number of clients to create a hand-built, one-of-one vehicle with MSRP starting at approximately $158,000.

Awards and Reviews

The Drive has given the recently updated CT5-V Blackwing two honors, Best Performance Car and Best Car of 2026.

The 2026 Cadillac CT5 V-Series Blackwing is the only pure, raw (yet comfortable) ultra-high-performance sedan left on the market. The Precision Package makes the Blackwing a world-class super-sedan and perhaps the best-driving four-door production car on the planet.

Future V-Series Lineup

CT5 Successor—Rumor/Single Source: Insider sources tell us that GM will indeed build a second-generation Cadillac CT5-V Blackwing high-performance sedan. The next-gen CT5 is set to ride on the updated Alpha 2-2 platform. The upcoming Chevy Camaro will share the same A2-2 platform with the CT5-V Blackwing and will have a high-performance model and offer manual transmission.

CT4 Discontinued: While the larger CT5 will return for a second generation, the smaller CT4 has been discontinued and will not be making a comeback in North America.

Electrified V-Series Expansion

In 2025, Cadillac V-Series enjoyed its best sales year ever and the introduction of its first electrified models, the OPTIQ-V and LYRIQ-V.

Lyriq-V: The 2026 Cadillac Lyriq-V is available now in U.S. showrooms with MSRP starting at $80,090. With an extra 100 horsepower and 200 pound-feet of torque, the Lyriq-V offers serious acceleration, hitting 60 mph from a standstill in just over three seconds.

Optiq-V: The 2026 Cadillac Optiq V-Series backs up its steep $79,270 price tag with blistering 519-horsepower acceleration.
