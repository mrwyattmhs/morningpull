Enthusiast car market

Auction Highlights & Record Prices

Mecum's Kissimmee event in January set a new record with $441 million in total sales across nearly two weeks, with 54 lots crossing the million-dollar mark, headlined by a white 1962 Ferrari 250 GTO that sold for $38.5 million. Monterey Car Week 2026 auctions totaled a record $748 million.

Recent standout results include: a 2026 Chevrolet Corvette ZR1 Stars & Steel Edition convertible (one of only three built) bringing $561,000 with commission, a 1993 Porsche 911 Carrera RSR 3.8 reaching £3,492,500 (about $4.67M), and a 1994 Jaguar XJ220 bringing £506,250.

Market Splits: Strong Top, Soft Middle

Auctions in early 2026 show enthusiasm for high-end cars appealing to younger demographics, but many cars in the same sales produced ho-hum results with an overall slight downward trend. The lower end weakens as buyers face economic pressure, while wealthy collectors remain unaffected and eager to spend.

Values have largely normalized across the board, but certain classics appreciate even as the broader market flattens, driven by generational preference—Gen X and older millennials buying cars they grew up wanting.

Gaining Value

Nissan Skyline GT-R, Porsche Carrera GT, C6 Corvette Z06, 1990s BMW M cars, air-cooled and early water-cooled Porsches, and clean Japanese performance cars continue to attract attention. The Porsche Carrera GT's average value is expected to soon reach $2M or more. The sixth-gen Honda Civic Si has seen its average #2 condition value rise to $33,400, up from $15,500 in 2016.

Porsche 911 (993 and 997 generations), Acura NSX, Dodge Viper, BMW M3, and various air-cooled and water-cooled sports cars continue attracting buyers seeking engaging driving without modern complexity.

Modern Performance Trending Toward Collectibility

No segment has shown more resilience than analog performance cars from the 1980s, 1990s, and early 2000s. Collectors increasingly view these cars as the final generation before advanced driver assistance systems, digital interfaces, and electrification transformed the driving experience.

Seven of the year's top 10 sales are expected to be post-1990 vehicles, with six of the top 10 from 2025 built after 1990 and that number expected to grow in 2026.

Toyota Supras, Nissan Skylines, Mazda RX-7s, Acura Integras, Mitsubishi Evolutions, Subaru WRXs, and Honda performance models continue generating strong interest from younger collectors.

Manual transmission performance cars

NEW MODELS & ANNOUNCEMENTS

Porsche confirmed an MT Package for the 911 Carrera S — bringing back the six-speed manual after dropping it from that trim at the end of the 2025 model year — and McLaren revealed its McL 6GT supercar will be offered with a manual, the first McLaren production car to feature one since the F1 of the early 1990s. The Porsche 911 Carrera S with MT Package costs over $167,000 — roughly $20,000 more than the PDK-equipped Carrera S.

In June 2026, Subaru announced a trio of new manual-equipped vehicles, headlined by a resurrected TY85 gearbox for the WRX — the same heavier-duty unit that once underpinned the Impreza WRX STi.

DISCONTINUATIONS

The manuals being discontinued after the 2026 model year include the Volkswagen Jetta GLI, BMW M3/M4, BMW Z4, Toyota GR Supra, Lotus Emira, Cadillac CT5-V Blackwing, and Cadillac CT4-V Blackwing.

Volkswagen recently confirmed that starting with the 2027 model year, the Jetta GLI will exclusively feature the 7-speed DSG dual-clutch transmission. That makes the 2026 Jetta GLI the absolute final new Volkswagen you can buy with three pedals in the United States.

The current-generation 718 Cayman and Boxster end production in 2026, with replacement models expected to be electric-only or hybrid.

SURVIVAL & STRONG DEMAND

Within that tiny sliver of the market, something interesting is happening: the people who can still get manuals are choosing them at rates that would shock anyone who believes "nobody wants manuals anymore." Manuals are disappearing not because enthusiasts abandoned them, but because manufacturers decided the economics don't justify offering them across entire lineups.

According to Motor1's comprehensive annual survey of automaker manual transmission sales for 2025, Subaru BRZ: 90% of buyers chose the manual; Subaru WRX: 85% manual take rate; Porsche 911 GT3 Touring: 83% manual; Toyota GR Corolla: 71% manual; Cadillac CT4-V Blackwing: Over 60% manual; Toyota GR Supra: 56% manual (final year).

Subaru and Toyota together account for nearly half of all manual-equipped new cars available in the US market — a remarkable concentration that reflects both brands' deliberate commitment to driver engagement across multiple model lines.

MARKET CONTEXT

Just 0.6% of new vehicles made for U.S. customers were stick shifts in 2025. Only 24 vehicles sold in the United States still offer a manual gearbox option for 2026. That's down from over 35 models just five years ago.

Track driving & car control

TRACK DRIVING & HIGH-PERFORMANCE EDUCATION

Technique & Fundamentals

Drivers learn the fundamentals of vehicle dynamics, racing lines, braking, cornering, and throttle control in courses matched to their experience level. Key factors include weight distribution, tire grip, and suspension geometry, with weight transfer affecting handling during acceleration and braking. Proper driving line, vision, and situational awareness are emphasized across HPDE curricula.

Tires for Track Use

Track-specific tires are the most important parts for safety and reliability; generally, DOT-legal tires with a treadwear rating at or under 200 TW are the standard for track tires, though good tires with adequate tread work for beginners. For first few track days, high-performance summer tires or entry-level R-compounds give predictable behavior. Check tire pressure repeatedly and note wear patterns; before the track, ensure adequate tread and bring a tire pressure gauge.

Brakes & Brake Setup

Replace worn pads and consider using high-temperature fluid; keep sessions short to prevent brake overheating. Brake pads should have at least 50% material remaining. For regular track use, upgrade to performance pads; track driving can exceed standard DOT 4's boiling point, so flush and replace with high-temperature DOT 4+ (260°C+) or racing DOT 4 (300°C+). New pads and rotors need a bedding procedure following pad manufacturer instructions; between sessions, give brakes time to cool and avoid aggressive on-off usage to preserve performance.

Data & Coaching Tools

Professional instructors provide personalized guidance and valuable insights during lapping sessions to help refine driving technique. Elite in-car coaching and in-depth classroom instruction come from top professionals in driver training programs. Organizations increasingly offer structured telemetry and lap-time analysis within HPDE programs, though few new tools were prominently detailed in current materials.

Notable Events 2026

Hooked on Driving runs HPDE events at over 25+ track venues coast-to-coast including Watkins Glen, Laguna Seca, and Thunderhill. Pocono Raceway will host two HPDE Track Days in 2026: July 8th and September 9th. Autobahn Country Club hosts Round #7 of TREC offering 3-hour HPDE, time trial, sprint racing, endurance racing, and open track on September 11–13, 2026. NASA Championships at Sebring International run September 23–27, 2026, featuring time trial and sprint racing. FARA's Super Lap Series offers time-trial-style competition for modified street cars with track-focused modifications like coilovers, performance tires, and bucket seats.

Car Preparation Checklist

Checking elements like tires, brakes, and fluids is essential before going to the track. Check that lug nuts are torqued to spec; tires should have plenty of life with no chords showing. Increased negative camber helps keep the tire's contact patch flat during hard cornering, improving grip and reducing outer-edge tire wear; even modest adjustment makes a noticeable difference in front-end grip. Start with moderate pressure +6–8 psi over street pressure, conservative camber around -1°, and stock anti-roll bars for a baseline session.
