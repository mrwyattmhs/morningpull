Enthusiast car market

COLLECTOR CAR MARKET NEWS

MONTEREY 2026: RECORD SPENDING AT THE TOP

The various collector car auctions held during Monterey Car Week set a new record with around $748 million spent — an increase of over $315 million from a year ago and over $277 million more than the previous high set in 2022.

Headline results: - A 2026 Ferrari Luce 'Tailor Made' sold for $40 million, setting records for the most expensive electric car and most expensive automotive charity lot ever. - A 1964 Shelby Cobra Daytona Coupe took $42,905,000, a record for an American car at auction. - A 1996 McLaren F1 GTR sold for almost $35 million — a new marque and British-built car record. - The most expensive car sold at the Mecum Monterey 2026 auction was a 1996 Ferrari F50 for a model record of $14,575,000.

69 lots exceeded $1 million, 14 surpassed $5 million, six crossed $10 million and two exceeded $30 million.

TWO-TIER MARKET: STRENGTH AT TOP, WEAKNESS BELOW

Auctions show plenty of enthusiasm on high-end cars that skew to a young demographic, however a large volume of cars sold in those same sales have produced ho-hum results, with an overall slight downward trend. Mass-market collectibles are generally falling in value as the cost of ownership increases.

Cars from the 1980s through the 2020s carried median premiums of 31 to 38% over Hagerty Price Guide condition-appropriate values, while cars from the 1960s were a median 6.2% under once the Cobra Daytona Coupe is excluded.

MODERN PERFORMANCE CARS DRIVING COLLECTIBILITY

Modern classics and 1980s–1990s vehicles keep drawing younger buyers chasing the cars of their youth, and that generational handoff is reshaping which models command attention.

Key performers emerging as collectibles:

  • As modern performance cars move towards turbocharging and electrification, the S2000's purity has made it increasingly collectible, with unmodified, low-mileage examples already rising in value.
  • The Porsche Carrera GT is the obvious example, with a recent Amelia auction result seeing one sell for around $6.7 million.
  • Values for the Lamborghini Aventador SVJ have begun to strengthen as the wider market recognises that this particular era of Lamborghini V12 road car is unlikely to return.
  • The Toyota GR Corolla, Hyundai Veloster N, and Subaru WRX hatchback are among the most promising candidates for affordable future collectibles.

Modern enthusiast cars from the 1990s and 2000s—especially cars with analog appeal, manual transmissions, and limited electronic interference—are still rising.

Manual transmission performance cars

Let me search for more recent reviews and takes on manual transmission cars:## Manual Transmission News

Major Announcements (This Week)

Porsche confirmed an MT Package for the 911 Carrera S—bringing back the six-speed manual after dropping it from that trim at the end of the 2025 model year—and McLaren revealed its McL 6GT supercar will be offered with a manual, the first McLaren production car to feature one since the F1 of the early 1990s.

The Cutoff Looms

Only 24 vehicles sold in the United States still offer a manual gearbox option, down from over 35 models just five years ago. The 2026 Toyota GR Supra "Final Edition" confirms this is the last year for the manual Supra. Production of the 718 Boxster and Cayman ends in 2026.

Who's Holding the Line

A small group of automakers—Porsche, McLaren, Subaru, Toyota, Ford, and BMW—are actively committing to the stick shift in 2026 and beyond, even as Volkswagen, GM, and others quietly phase it out. Mazda continues to champion the manual car with the Miata and Mazda's lightweight sports offerings.

Why Manuals Persist

Within a tiny market sliver, people who can still get manuals are choosing them at surprisingly high rates; manuals are disappearing not because enthusiasts abandoned them, but because manufacturers decided the economics don't justify offering them across entire lineups. Hybridization fundamentally conflicts with manual transmissions—electric motors provide instant torque that automatic transmissions manage more effectively, making manuals technically impractical as hybridization spreads.

Notable Models Still Available

The 2026 GR86 comes standard with a 6-speed manual transmission. The 2026 Toyota GR Corolla features a 1.6-liter turbo three-cylinder, standard all-wheel drive, and a six-speed manual. The 2026 BMW lineup still features manual transmission options for its M2, M3 and M4 series performance sedans and the Z-series roadster.
